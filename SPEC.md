### Introduction

WardSathi is a mobile application designed to make ward office services in Nepal easier to access. Users can view service requirements, required documents, fees, and processing details. The app also allows citizens to submit applications, upload documents, and track their application status through one simple platform.

### Problem Statement

Many citizens in Nepal visit ward offices without knowing the exact documents, fees, or procedures required. This can cause repeated visits, long waiting times, and confusion. WardSathi addresses this problem by providing clear service information and allowing users to track their applications digitally.

### Objectives

The main objective of WardSathi is to simplify access to local government services. The app aims to provide service information, document checklists, online application submission, and status tracking. It also includes an admin dashboard for ward staff to review applications and update their progress.
